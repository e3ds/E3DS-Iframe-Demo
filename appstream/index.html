What AppStream does not do (Pixel Streaming apps can):
  • No messages between the page and the app's own code - the app has no plugin, so sendMessageToStreamingBuild and replies from the app do not exist.
  • The page cannot press keys in the app for the visitor (emulateKeyboardKey*): only the visitor's own keyboard, mouse and touch reach it.
  • No console commands, device / app info, screenshot, volume or "end session" commands from the page - this page ends a session by closing the stream frame.
  • Resolution follows the streaming machine, not the size of this frame.
